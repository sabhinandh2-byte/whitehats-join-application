Identity verification provider IDScan recently got hit with a massive breach where hackers accessed their cloud storage and walked away with over 150 million records, including driver's licenses and passport details. What stood out to me about this incident is the sheer irony and danger of third-party risk—companies we trust specifically to verify and protect our identities are often just one cloud misconfiguration away from exposing everything.
